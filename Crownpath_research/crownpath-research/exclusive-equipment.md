Scope: Exclusive Equipment options that **change the kit** — **strip / dispel**, **extra turn**, **Dual Attack**, **Injury**, **Combat Readiness**. Ignore pure Atk%/HP%/Speed%/damage% EE. No scout recipes. No wall-type guesses. No meta bible.

Keywords locked: **Combat Readiness** (not turn bar). **Extra Attack** is not **Dual Attack**. **Seal** is not **Cannot Buff**. **CR cut** is not **Speed cap**.
