Keywords locked: **Combat Readiness** (not turn bar). **Extra Attack** is not **Dual Attack**. **Seal** is not **Cannot Buff**. **Combat Readiness cut** is not a **Speed cap**. Harsetti’s PvP limit is a **Speed cap** (90% of her Speed), separate from CR cut.
