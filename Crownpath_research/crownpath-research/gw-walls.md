## How to read this

- **Match count** = how often that exact triple appears in uploaded Ark data (all-time filter).
- **Def WR%** = defense win rate in that sample (higher = harder for attackers in the tracker).
- “Skip wajar?” = reasonable to walk if you lack a real answer — **not** a force/skip oracle for every league.
- Patterns group the Ark triples; example units are the **exact** tracker lineups.
